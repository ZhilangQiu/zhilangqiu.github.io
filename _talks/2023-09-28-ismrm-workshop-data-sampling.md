Invited talk at the ISMRM Workshop on Data Sampling & Image Reconstruction. Presented the self-calibrated two-step reconstruction framework for multidimensional MR fingerprinting, highlighting how temporally local matrix completion can estimate the temporal subspace directly from k-space center data without navigators or external calibration.
