Abstract #7528. Oral Presentation. Presented the self-calibrated subspace reconstruction approach for multidimensional MR fingerprinting enabling simultaneous quantification of T1, T2, and diffusion parameters. This work demonstrated reliable parameter maps without pulsation gating by using temporally local matrix completion for automatic phase error detection and correction. The method was subsequently published in *Magnetic Resonance in Medicine* (2024).
