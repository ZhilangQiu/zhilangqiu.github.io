Oral presentation at the ISMRM Workshop on Data Sampling & Imaging Reconstruction. Presented the self-calibrated two-step reconstruction framework for multidimensional MR fingerprinting (mdMRF), detailing how temporally local matrix completion on k-space center data provides self-calibrated temporal subspace estimation without external navigators. The work was later published in *Magnetic Resonance in Medicine* (2024).
