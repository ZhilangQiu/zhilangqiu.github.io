Presented the self-calibrated subspace reconstruction approach for multidimensional MR fingerprinting (mdMRF). The method enables simultaneous quantification of T1, T2, and diffusion parameters with automatic phase error correction from bulk and physiological motions, without requiring pulsation gating. This work was subsequently published in *Magnetic Resonance in Medicine* (2024).
