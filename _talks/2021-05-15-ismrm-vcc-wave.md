Abstract #1159. Presented the VCC-Wave method combining virtual conjugate coil reconstruction and wave encoding for improved parallel MRI acceleration. This work demonstrated 6-fold acceleration with good image quality in high-resolution and high-bandwidth scenarios, and was later published in *Magnetic Resonance in Medicine* (2021).
