Abstract #0627. Oral Presentation. Presented an explicit phase error modeling framework for multidimensional MR fingerprinting (mdMRF) to improve robustness in simultaneous T1, T2, and diffusion mapping. The approach models phase errors from bulk motion and physiological pulsation within the MRF signal model, enabling robust parameter estimation without pulsation gating or navigator echoes.
