Program #551-03-006. Power Pitch presentation. Presented work on using DTI-ALPS (diffusion tensor image analysis along the perivascular space) to quantify glymphatic function and characterize dysfunction and age-related trajectories across neuropsychiatric disorders including schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and depression. This work is conducted at McLean Hospital / Harvard Medical School.

**Award**: Second Place, MR in Psychiatry Study Group Award, ISMRM 2026.
