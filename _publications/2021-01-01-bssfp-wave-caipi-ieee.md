This paper proposes a modified Wave-CAIPI technique with truncated wave gradients for accelerating 3D balanced steady-state free precession (bSSFP) imaging. The truncated wave gradients reduce the transition bandwidth effects on bSSFP contrast while maintaining the g-factor improvement of Wave-CAIPI encoding. Su S and Qiu Z contributed equally to this work.
