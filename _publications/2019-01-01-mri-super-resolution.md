This paper proposes a cross-modal edge-preserving regularization approach for robust MR image super-resolution reconstruction. By incorporating structural edge information from complementary MR contrast images as a regularization prior, the method achieves improved super-resolution quality, particularly for preserving fine anatomical structures and edges.
