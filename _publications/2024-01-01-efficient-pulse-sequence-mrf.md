This paper proposes an efficient pulse sequence design framework for high-dimensional MR fingerprinting (MRF). For effective optimization of MRF pulse sequences, estimating and minimizing errors from actual scan conditions are crucial. The proposed Systematic Error Index (SEI) provides a computationally efficient metric for pulse sequence optimization, avoiding the need for lengthy dictionary simulations during the design phase.
