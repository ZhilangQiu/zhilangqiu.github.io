A self-calibrated two-step subspace reconstruction for multidimensional MR fingerprinting (mdMRF) is proposed. Step 1 (self-calibration) reconstructs aliasing-free, low-resolution, time-resolved images using temporally local matrix completion from k-space center data. Step 2 (subspace reconstruction) reconstructs high-resolution images using temporally global subspace reconstruction, with the temporal subspace estimated from Step 1. This approach enables simultaneous quantification of T1, T2, and diffusion parameters in a single scan, and automatically detects and corrects phase errors from bulk and physiological motions without pulsation gating.
