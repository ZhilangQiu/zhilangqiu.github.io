This paper presents a rapid variable flip angle approach for positive susceptibility contrast imaging of clinical metal seeds. By exploiting the susceptibility-induced frequency shifts around metallic implants, the method generates positive (bright) contrast around metal rather than the conventional signal voids, facilitating reliable detection and localization of brachytherapy seeds and other clinical metallic devices.
