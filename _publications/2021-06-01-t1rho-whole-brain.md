This paper develops a multislice T1ρ mapping solution (MS-T1ρ) for robust and accelerated whole-brain T1ρ quantification. The method is based on a two-acquisition scheme using turbo spin echo with RF cycling, enabling whole-brain coverage with 0.8-mm in-plane resolution within approximately 4 minutes—a clinically feasible scan time. T1ρ relaxation time is a sensitive biomarker for a range of neurological and musculoskeletal conditions.
