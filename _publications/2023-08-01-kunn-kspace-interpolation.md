K-UNN proposes using an untrained (randomly initialized) neural network for k-space interpolation in accelerated MRI reconstruction. By leveraging the implicit regularization properties of neural network architectures, the method achieves competitive image quality without requiring a large matched training dataset, making it applicable in scenarios with limited training data.
