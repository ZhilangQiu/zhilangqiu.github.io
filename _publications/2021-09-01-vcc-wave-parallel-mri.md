This paper proposes VCC-Wave, a novel model incorporating virtual conjugate coil (VCC) reconstruction and wave encoding for improved parallel MRI. The encoding capability is analyzed using the correlation matrix of the encoding operator. In vivo experiments demonstrate good image quality at 6-fold acceleration in high-resolution and high-bandwidth cases, indicating improvement over the original Wave technique. Zhilang Qiu and Sen Jia contributed equally to this work.
