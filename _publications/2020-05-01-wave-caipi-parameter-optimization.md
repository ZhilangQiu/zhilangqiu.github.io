This paper proposes a parameter optimization framework for the wave gradients of Wave-CAIPI imaging to decrease g-factor penalty and reduce reconstruction artifacts. The influences of key parameters (amplitude, frequency, and number of sinusoidal cycles) on g-factor are theoretically analyzed. The average g-factor is used as an optimization metric, and a fast calculation method is proposed for practical implementation. Haifeng Wang and Zhilang Qiu contributed equally to this work.
