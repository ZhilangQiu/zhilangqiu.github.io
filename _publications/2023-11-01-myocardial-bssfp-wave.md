This paper proposes a high-resolution single-shot myocardial imaging technique by combining balanced steady-state free precession (bSSFP) with wave encoding. Wave encoding enables highly accelerated parallel imaging with reduced g-factor penalty, allowing high spatial resolution cardiac imaging within a single heartbeat and minimizing motion artifacts.
