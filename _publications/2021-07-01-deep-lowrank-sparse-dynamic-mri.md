This paper proposes L+S-Net, a deep unrolling network based on the low-rank plus sparse (L+S) decomposition framework (also known as robust PCA) for dynamic MRI reconstruction. The network addresses key limitations of conventional compressed sensing MRI reconstruction, including empirical parameter selection and limited acceleration rates. By unrolling the iterative L+S algorithm into a deep network, the method learns optimal parameters end-to-end from training data.
