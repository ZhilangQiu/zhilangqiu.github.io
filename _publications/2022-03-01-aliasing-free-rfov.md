This paper proposes an aliasing-free reduced field-of-view (rFOV) parallel imaging method. The proposed approach addresses the wrap-around aliasing artifacts that appear in conventional rFOV acquisitions, and is applicable to both traditional Cartesian parallel imaging and advanced Wave-encoded acquisitions. Sen Jia and Zhilang Qiu contributed equally to this work.
