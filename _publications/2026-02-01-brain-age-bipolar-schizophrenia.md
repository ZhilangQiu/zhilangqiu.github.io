This study applies a 3D convolutional neural network to estimate structural brain age from T1-weighted MRI in patients with bipolar disorder and schizophrenia. Grad-CAM attribution maps are used to identify brain regions driving the age predictions. The work is conducted at McLean Hospital and provides pilot evidence for brain age gap as a neurobiological marker in these psychiatric disorders.
